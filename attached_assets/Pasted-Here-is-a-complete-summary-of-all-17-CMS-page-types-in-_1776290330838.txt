Here is a complete summary of all 17 CMS page types in the **Karl content dashboard** (SF.gov Wagtail CMS), structured for use by your AI agent:

---

## SF.gov Wagtail CMS — Page Type Structure Reference

**Platform:** Wagtail CMS | **Dashboard:** Karl content dashboard | **Base URL:** `https://api.sf.gov/admin/`

**Universal fields present on ALL page types (via the Promote tab):**
- Slug* (required, URL path)
- Title tag (SEO headline)
- Meta description (SEO description)
- Show in menus (checkbox)
- Tags (comma-separated)
- Publishing schedule: Go live date/time, Expiry date/time

---

### 1. About Us (`/admin/pages/add/sf/about/2/`)
A page for explaining an organization's background and resources.

**Content Tab Fields:**
- Title* (required)
- Primary agency* (page chooser, sf.agency)
- Information (StreamField / block list)
- Resources (StreamField / block list)

---

### 2. Agency (`/admin/pages/add/sf/agency/2/`)
The main landing page for a City agency. The most complex page type.

**Content Tab Fields:**
- Title* (short, sentence case; optional "show on agency list" toggle)
- Description
- Logo (image, min 100×100px, square, white/transparent background)
- Main image (image, min 375px wide, horizontal)
- Alert (short text, <65 chars)
- Quick links
- Meeting information
- Section title 1* + Subsection
- Spotlight 1, Spotlight 2
- Section title 2* + Subsection
- About (rich text body)
- Call to action
- Divisions or subcommittees (page chooser, sub-agencies)
- Partner agencies (page chooser)
- People
- Public records*
- Archive information (Archive URL, Archive date)
- Meeting archive information (Meeting archive date, Meeting archive URL)
- Contact us (Address, Phone, Email, Social media/other)
- Redirect this page to
- Topics

## Key Patterns & Notes for Your Agent

**Common structural conventions across all page types:**
1. **Title*** is always required and is the public-facing page title (except News which uses "Headline*" and Profile which uses "Name*").
2. **Primary agency*** is required on all page types except Agency itself — it's a page chooser limited to `sf.agency` model.
3. **Partner agencies** appears on most pages and controls which agency pages surface that content.
4. **Topics** is a tagging/cross-reference field on event, news, information, transaction, step-by-step, resource collection, and topic pages.
5. **Contact us** (Address, Phone, Email, Social media) is a reusable block on agency, campaign, event, location, profile, and transaction.
6. **Rich text** fields use Draftail editor with support for Heading 3, Heading 4, Blockquote, and inline blocks triggered by `/`.
7. **Redirect this page to** exists on agency, news, profile, and transaction — useful for archiving or consolidating content.
8. All pages share the same **SEO/Promote tab** (slug, title tag, meta description, show in menus, tags) and **publishing schedule** (go live/expiry datetime).
9. The CMS is a **Wagtail v7.0.6** instance scoped to the `sf` app namespace, hosted at `api.sf.gov`.
