Apply for a business permit

Get the permit you need to legally operate a storefront or commercial business in San Francisco.

Description

Most businesses operating in San Francisco need a permit before opening. The application process takes 10–15 business days and can be completed online or in person at City Hall.

What to know

Before applying, make sure you meet the eligibility requirements and have all required documents ready. Incomplete applications will be returned and may delay your opening date.

  • Your business must be located in San Francisco city limits
  • All business owners listed must be 18 or older
  • A valid federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) is required
  • Zoning approval may be needed for certain business types

If your business involves food service, alcohol, or healthcare, additional permits from state agencies may be required before your city permit is approved.

What to do

Gather your documents, then submit your application online. You will receive a confirmation email within one business day.

  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • Proof of business address (lease or deed)
  • Federal EIN letter from the IRS
  • Completed SF business registration form
